## Definition used

**First-year sales = the first full fiscal year of sales (April–March, Indian FY), domestic units.**

This matters: a vehicle launched in January (Xylo) has only ~3 months in FY2008-09, so its first *full* year is FY2009-10. The spec panel label must say "first-year units" and the tooltip or caption should carry the fiscal year, so the figure can't be read as a launch-quarter number.

## Per-vehicle detail

### Mahindra Maxx: 2001
Launched 2001 in nine- or ten-seat station wagon form, replacing the Mahindra Armada. Later produced as a pickup.
**Sales:** not published per model.
⚠️ Do not confuse with the **Bolero Maxx Pik-Up** (2022), which is a different nameplate.
Source: [Wikipedia, Mahindra Maxx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Maxx)

### Bolero XL: 2001
The Bolero nameplate launched in 2000 (reports differ on April vs 4 August). The **XL** variant, 9/10 seats, aimed at the taxi segment, appears from 2001; CarDekho catalogues it as "Bolero 2001–2010 XL 9 Seater".
**Sales:** never split from the Bolero total, so no variant-level figure exists publicly.
Sources: [Wikipedia, Mahindra Bolero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Bolero) · [CarDekho, Bolero 2001–2010 XL 9 Seater](https://www.cardekho.com/overview/Mahindra_Bolero_2001-2010/Mahindra_Bolero_2001-2010_XL_9_Seater_Non_AC.htm) · [Times Now, Bolero 25-year timeline](https://www.timesnownews.com/auto/car-news/mahindra-boleros-evolution-25-year-journey-of-the-peoples-suv-article-152963293)

### Xylo: 13 January 2009
Launched at Nashik; retail sales began March 2009. 3,171 units in the first full month, 15,000+ in the first six months, 7,800 units in June 2009 alone. FY2009-10 closed at **27,978** units, against 32,062 in FY2010-11. Production ran 2009–2019.
Sources: [Wikipedia, Mahindra Xylo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Xylo) · [Goa on Wheels, Xylo sells over 32,000](https://www.goaonwheels.com/mahindra-xylo-sells-over-32000-units) · [DNA, Jul 2009 (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130325212222/http://www.dnaindia.com/money/report_m-and-m-net-soars-152pct-as-xylo-picks-up-speed_1278579)

### Maxximo: 3 February 2010
Greenfield programme: unveiled at Auto Expo 2010, commercially launched 3 Feb 2010, first product from the Chakan plant. Launch production 2,000 units/month, scalable to 3,000. Crossed 100,000 domestic units by 2013 and 240,000 across the platform in five years. Discontinued 2015.
**Sales:** no first-year figure published, only cumulative milestones.
Sources: [The Auto Channel, launch, Rs 2.79 lakh](https://www.theautochannel.com/news/2010/02/04/464610.html) · [Autocar Professional, 240,000 in five years](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/mahindra-maxximo-mini-truck-sells-240-units-8005) · [Wikipedia, Mahindra Maxximo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Maxximo)

### Mahindra Jeeto: 23 June 2015
Modular range of 8 mini-trucks, built at Zaheerabad. **20,569** units in FY2015-16, its first year, beating the Tata Ace on price. 100,000 Jeeto Load units by 2019; 200,000 platform units by 2022.
Sources: [Autocar Professional, Jeeto vs Ace, FY2015-16 sales](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/-mahindra-jeeto-mini-truck-sells-unit-months-25103) · [Wikipedia, Mahindra Jeeto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_Jeeto) · [Mahindra press release, Jeeto Plus](https://www.mahindra.com/news-room/press-release/en/mahindra-introduces-new-and-advanced-variant-jeeto-plus-to-its-mini-truck-range) · [Autocar Professional, 200,000 units](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/mahindra-jeeto-sales-cross-200-000-units-103091)

### Tata Signa: February 2016
Range unveiled at SIAM Auto Expo 2016 with three variants: 4923.S tractor, 3118.T multi-axle truck, 2518.K tipper. Commercial launch announced September 2016.
**Variant question:** the supplied asset is a **Signa 4825.TK**, a later model (c. 2020). Decide whether the entry describes the 2016 range launch or the 4825.TK specifically, they have different years.
**Sales:** Tata does not publish model-level sales.
Sources: [Automotive Fleet, launch, 5 Feb 2016](https://www.automotive-fleet.com/news/tata-launches-signa-commercial-vehicles) · [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/tata-motors-launches-new-signa-range-commercial-vehicles) · [ET Auto, commercial launch, Sep 2016](https://auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/tata-motors-launches-new-signa-range-of-mhcvs/54374944)

## Gaps and what would close them

| Gap | Why it exists | What would close it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maxx, Bolero XL, Maxximo, Signa first-year sales | Indian OEMs publish model-level volumes to SIAM, not to the public; pre-2010 press coverage rarely reported them | A SIAM subscription, or Mahindra/Tata internal launch decks, i.e. **the owner's own records** |
| Bolero XL as a distinct model | Mahindra reported Bolero as one line | Owner confirmation of which variant he led and whether it should be its own entry |
| Signa variant | Asset shows 4825.TK (c. 2020); range launched 2016 | Owner confirmation |
| Maxx / Bolero XL exact launch dates | Only year-level sourcing found | Owner confirmation, or Mahindra press archive |

## Consequence for the design

Only **2 of 6** vehicles have a first-year sales figure. The spec panel in PRD §5.2.4 therefore needs an explicit empty state, a dash with the label "first-year sales" and a quiet note such as *"not published"*, rather than a blank space or a zero. A dash is honest; a zero is a claim.
